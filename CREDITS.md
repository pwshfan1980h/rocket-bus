## Music and sound effects

All music (`assets/music/*.ogg`) and most sound effects (`assets/audio/*.wav`) were
generated with ElevenLabs (Eleven Music and Sound Effects) on a paid Creator plan.
The prompts are in `tools/gen_elevenlabs.py` and `docs/audio.md`. Chiptune UI blips,
jingles and voice blips are still synthesized by `tools/gen_audio.py`.

Licensing (checked 2026-11-19):
- Sound effects: commercial use in games is allowed on paid plans, as part of the game,
  not as standalone sample files. ElevenCreative Terms, Exhibit 4: https://elevenlabs.io/creative-terms
- Music: Creator plan allows commercial use except film, TV, radio and "Studio Games". An
  "Indie Game" (under $500k lifetime game revenue, developer under $1M a year) is not a
  Studio Game. No attribution required. Music Terms, Exhibit A: https://elevenlabs.io/music-terms
- Creator is "for individual use only". Re-check the terms if the game is published through
  a company or passes those revenue limits.
